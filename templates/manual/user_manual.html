KPIMaster Operations & Settings Manual

Standard Operating Procedures, Role-Specific Entry Workflows, Scoring Rules & Setup Guidelines

Employee Daily Work Entry & Task Guide
Important Rule for Employees: You do not need to calculate or submit your monthly KPI score. Simply submit your actual daily work output accurately. The system automatically computes your KPI achievement based on your supervisor's verified entries.
How to Submit Daily Work
  1. Click Daily Work Entry in the sidebar.
  2. Click the blue "+ New Daily Entry" button.
  3. Work Date: Select today's date (or the date the work was performed).
  4. Assigned KPI: Select the relevant KPI indicator assigned to your role (e.g. [HROP-Budget] Absenteeism Control).
  5. Task Category: Select KPI Direct Task, Routine Task, or Assigned Task.
  6. Priority: Choose Low, Medium, High, or Critical.
  7. Target Qty vs Achieved Qty: Enter the target expected (e.g. 1.00) and your actual output (e.g. 1.00).
  8. Upload Evidence: Attach your supporting Excel sheet, scan, PDF report, or image (Max 10MB).
  9. Click "Submit Daily Work".
Managing Assigned Tasks & Statuses
  1. Go to Work Operations → My Tasks.
  2. View tasks assigned directly by your Supervisor or Department Head.
  3. Pay attention to the Deadline, Priority Level, and Target Points.
  4. Once completed, submit a daily work entry selecting the assigned task title to claim achievement.
  5. Track approval feedback under Performance Review → View Card.

Supervisor Task Verification & Scoring Guide

Supervisors are the quality gatekeepers of the KPI scoring pipeline. Employee claimed achievements are never automatically trusted; the calculation engine strictly uses the supervisor's Verified Achievement.

Verification Workflow Steps
  1. Open Work Operations → Work Approvals.
  2. You will see submissions from all staff reporting directly to you.
  3. Click "Verify & Score" on an item.
  4. Verified Achievement: Review evidence and enter the verified output (e.g. Employee claimed 25, you verify 22).
  5. Quality Score (30%): Rate output quality from 0 to 100.
  6. Timeliness Score (20%): Rate execution timeliness from 0 to 100.
  7. Supervisor Score (10%): Rate discipline and effort from 0 to 100.
  8. Select decision (Approve, Partially Approve, Return, or Reject).
  9. Click "Save Assessment".
Assigning Operational Tasks to Team
  1. Go to Work Operations → Assign Tasks.
  2. Enter the Task Title and clear instructions.
  3. Select the subordinate employee from your dropdown.
  4. Set the Start Date and Deadline Date.
  5. Assign target quantity, unit, and priority.
  6. Click "Assign Task". The task appears instantly on the employee's dashboard.
Department Head & Manager Settings Manual

Department Heads and Operating Managers configure the structural master parameters, KPI targets, weights, and multi-cycle review periods.

1. Organization Structure Master Setup

Access: Organization Structure in sidebar.

  • Departments: Add new departments with code and description (e.g. HRP - HR & Payroll).
  • Sections: Add sections attached to a parent department (e.g. PAY - Payroll Operations).
  • Sub-Sections: Add granular units (e.g. FSU - Final Settlement Unit).
  • Designations & Grades: Create job designations and grades (e.g. Executive - Payroll).
  • Floors & Locations: Map physical units (e.g. 3rd Floor, Gazipur Plant).
2. KPI Assignments & Weight Allocation

Access: KPI Configuration → Employee KPI Assignments.

  • Select the Employee, KPI Indicator, and Evaluation Cycle.
  • Enter the assigned Weight % and Target.
  • 100% Rule Validation: The system will block any assignment that causes the employee's total KPI weight to exceed 100.00%.
  • Configure Evaluation Periods (Weekly, Monthly, 3 Months, 6 Months, 9 Months, or Yearly).
HR Admin & Super Admin System Administration Manual
Employee Account Provisioning
  1. Open Organization → Employees → Add Employee.
  2. Fill in basic info: Employee Code, Name, Email, Mobile.
  3. Under System Login Credentials, check "Enable Login Account".
  4. Assign system role: SUPER_ADMIN, HR_ADMIN, DEPT_HEAD, SUPERVISOR, or EMPLOYEE.
  5. Link Department, Designation, and Direct Supervisor.
  6. Save. The employee can now log in using either their Username or Employee Code.
Password Reset & Score Override Rules
  1. Password Reset: Open any employee profile and click "Reset Login Password" to set new credentials without Django Admin.
  2. Manual Score Override: Open Performance Review → Edit on any record.
  3. Only HR and Super Admins can enable "Manual Score Override" with mandatory justification remarks.
  4. The original calculated score is permanently retained for audit compliance.

3. Period KPI Achievement KPI Achievement % = (Total Period Verified / Total Period Target) × 100 Per Assigned Indicator
4. Weighted KPI Score Weighted Score = (KPI Achievement % × Assigned Weight %) / 100 Summed to System Score
5. Final Performance Score Final Score = (System Generated Score × 80%) + (Supervisor Evaluation × 20%) 100% Overall
